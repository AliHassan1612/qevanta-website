Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before using Qevanta.

What is Qevanta?

Qevanta is a Chrome extension that helps you research public Instagram audiences, organize profiles, apply filters, and prepare outreach-ready lead lists.

Who is Qevanta built for?

Qevanta is built for agencies, freelancers, marketers, founders, consultants, and growth teams who need a faster way to organize prospect research.

Does Qevanta collect private data?

No. Qevanta is designed for public audience research workflows only. It does not unlock private profiles or access private information.

How do credits work?

Credits control monthly usage based on your plan. Free users get starter credits, while paid plans unlock larger limits, faster workflows, and advanced features.

How do clients install Qevanta?

Qevanta is available on the Chrome Web Store. Users can install it directly from the official listing and receive future updates automatically.

How does billing work?

Qevanta offers monthly and yearly plans. Paid subscriptions are processed securely through Stripe, and plan access is activated automatically after successful payment.
