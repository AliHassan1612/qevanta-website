Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What Qevanta Does

Qevanta helps users organize and analyze publicly visible audience signals for business research and lead discovery.

2. Public Data Only

Qevanta is designed to work with information that is publicly visible to the user in their browser. Users must not use Qevanta to access private accounts, bypass privacy controls, collect sensitive information, or violate platform rules.

3. Account Information

When you create an account, we may store your email address, name, company name, account status, plan, credit usage, referral code, and related profile information.

4. Authentication

Qevanta uses secure email authentication, verification emails, password recovery, and account sessions through our backend providers.

5. Local Browser Storage

The extension may store settings, analysis results, saved lists, extracted links, logs, and workflow data locally in your browser storage.

6. Payment and Billing

When paid plans are enabled, billing may be processed through a third-party payment provider. Qevanta does not store full card details directly.

7. Affiliate and Referral Tracking

If you use a referral or affiliate code, we may store the referral code, customer email, user ID, plan status, and commission-related records.

8. Data Exports

Users are responsible for how exported data is stored, used, contacted, shared, or deleted. Qevanta should not be used for spam, harassment, or unlawful activity.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect account and usage data. No online service can guarantee complete security.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Qevanta support through the official website.
