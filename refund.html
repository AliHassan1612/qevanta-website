1. Free Plan

The Free plan is provided without charge and therefore is not eligible for refunds.

Paid subscriptions give immediate access to premium features and usage credits. Because digital services are delivered instantly, payments are generally non-refundable.

3. Duplicate Charges

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same purchase, please contact us. Verified duplicate transactions will be refunded.

4. Billing Errors

If a payment was processed incorrectly because of a technical issue, we'll investigate the transaction and issue an appropriate refund when applicable.

5. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation prevents future renewals but does not automatically refund previous payments.

6. Abuse

Accounts suspended because of spam, fraud, abuse, or violation of our Terms of Service are not eligible for refunds.

7. Contact

If you have questions regarding billing or refunds, please contact Qevanta support through our official website.
